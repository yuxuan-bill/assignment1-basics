Transformer LM resource accounting (5 points):
a.
emb: vocab_size * d_model
one block:
 norm: 2 * d_model
 attn: 4 * d_model * d_model
 ffwd: 3 * d_model * d_ff
norm: d_model
linear: d_model * vocab_size
1640452800 params
1.6 GB for loading the model
b.
emb: no ops
block:
  norm: d_model * context_len * 6 * 2 (two norms)
  attn:
     kqv calc: d_model * d_model * context_len * 2 * 3
     rope: 8 * d_model / 2 * 2 (for q and k)
     kqv product: 2 * d_model * context_len * context_len (kq) + context_len * context_len (sqrt) + 2 * context_len * context_len (softmax) + context_len * context_len * d_model * 2 (kq * v) + 2 * context_len * d_model * d_model (kqv * o)
  ff: d_model * d_ff * context_len * 2 + d_ff * context_len * 4 + d_model * d_ff * context_len * 2 + d_ff * d_model * context_len * 2
  residual conn: 2 * d_model * context_len
norm: ignored
linear: 2 * d_model * vocab_size * context_len
so total only listing important ones
block:
attn: d_model ** 2 * context_len * 8 + context_len ** 2 * d_model * 4
ff: d_model * d_ff * context_len * 6
roughly 3.3trillion flops
c.
feed forward requires the most flops, then it is the attention calculation
d. d_model and layer size growth proportionally take more flops
e. flops increased by 256x and now the attn mechanism becomes the new bottleneck.


AdamW accounting:
a. 
input: 4 * d_model * vocab_size (params + adam_mv + gradient)
blocks:
norm (multiple by 2): 4 * d_model  (params + adam_mv + gradient) + batch_size * context_len * d_model (activation)
attention: d_model ** 2 * 4 * 4 (params + adam_mv + gradient) + context_len * batch_size * d_model * 5 (k,q,v,o + kq * v) + batch_size * num_heads * context_len ** 2 * 2(kq + softmax)
ff: d_model ** 2 * 8/3 * 3 * 4 (params + adam_mv + gradient) + context_len * batch_size * d_model * (1 + 4 * 8/3) (silu + (w1 * w3) @ w2)

final norm: 4 * d_model  (params + adam_mv + gradient) + batch_size * context_len * d_model (activation)
output: d_model * vocab_size * 4 (params + adam_mv + gradient) + batch_size * context_len * vocab_size(activation)
cross-entropy: 5 * batch_size * context_len * vocab_size

block_total: context_len * batch_size * d_model * (8 + 8/3 * 4) + batch_size * num_heads * context_len ** 2 * 2 + d_model ** 2 * (4 * 4 + 8/3 * 3 * 4) = (18.6 *  context_len * batch_size * d_model + d_model ** 2 * 48 + batch_size * num_heads * context_len ** 2 * 2) * num_layers * 4 (bytes)
input/output = 8 * 4 * d_model * vocab_size
total = (18.6 *  context_len * batch_size * d_model + d_model ** 2 * 48 + batch_size * num_heads * context_len ** 2 * 2) * num_layers * 4 + 32 * d_model * vocab_size

note: intentionally omitted calculations other than the ones in blocks and input/output, since they don't count a lot towards total. Also omitted 2 * 4 * d_model in pre_norm

16e9 * batch_size + 26e9, max batch size is 3

((d_model ** 2 * 4 + d_model ** 2 * 8/3 * 3) * num_blocks + d_model * vocab_size * 2) * 14
note: pre-norm is ignored
